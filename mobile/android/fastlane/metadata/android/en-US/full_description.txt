LanVid Monitor turns your Android phone into an ultra-low-latency personal in-ear monitor (IEM) mixer for LanVid Studio on Windows and macOS.

Mix up to 64 studio channels from your DAW in real time directly from the stage or rehearsal room — all heavy 64-to-stereo mixing and studio DSP runs on your PC while your phone receives a compact, rock-steady stereo stream over local Wi-Fi.

KEY FEATURES:
• Ultra-Low-Latency Native AAudio Engine: Direct C++ AAudio playback (5–20 ms Fixed buffer) with zero Java audio overhead and background playback with the screen off.
• Studio-Grade Opus CELT 48 kHz & Lossless PCM: Choose 128 / 192 / 256 kbps fullband Opus (20 Hz – 20 kHz) or Lossless 24-bit PCM on the fly.
• 64-Channel Personal Mix: Live VU meters, channel names from your PC, smooth click-free faders, stereo pan, Mute and Solo per channel — saved automatically per device.
• Server-Side Monitor FX DSP: High-Pass Filter (20–250 Hz), 3-band EQ (Low, sweepable Mid, High ±12 dB), Stereo Compressor with live Gain Reduction meter, Master Volume, and an ear-safe -0.5 dBFS Brickwall Limiter.
• Zero-Config LAN Discovery: Automatically finds LanVid Studio on your local Wi-Fi via mDNS (_lanvid._udp) or connects directly by IP.
• Built-in Demo Studio Preview: Explore all 8 channels, VU meters, and Monitor FX DSP offline without a PC.
• 100% Private & Local: No cloud servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads. All audio stays strictly inside your local network.
