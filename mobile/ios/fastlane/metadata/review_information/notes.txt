LanVid Monitor is a companion app for LanVid Studio, a desktop application (Windows / macOS / Linux) that streams audio over a local network. The app finds the studio on the same Wi-Fi network and plays a personal stereo monitor mix.

NO ACCOUNT OR LOGIN IS REQUIRED.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT A STUDIO (recommended):
1. Launch the app. On the first screen (Connect) tap "Open Demo Studio".
2. The Demo Studio is a simulated 8-channel studio (no network, no audio). Open the tabs:
   - Mixer: faders, pan, Mute/Solo, live level meters, stream quality and buffer.
   - Monitor FX: high-pass filter, 3-band EQ, compressor with Gain Reduction meter, limiter and volume.
   - Settings: device name, Privacy Policy and Support links, version, "Leave Demo Studio".
3. Tap "Disconnect" on the Connect tab or "Leave Demo Studio" in Settings to return to the normal screen.

LOCAL NETWORK PERMISSION: on the Connect screen the app browses for the Bonjour service _lanvid._udp to find LanVid Studio on the user's Wi-Fi, so iOS shows the Local Network permission prompt (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription). Denying it does not block the app: the Demo Studio and connecting by IP address stay available.

BACKGROUND AUDIO: the app declares the "audio" background mode so the monitor mix keeps playing in headphones while the screen is locked (it plays a live audio stream received from the studio).

PRIVACY: the app collects no data, has no accounts, no ads and no third-party SDKs; all traffic stays on the local network. Privacy Policy: https://manul02-ndn.github.io/lanvid/privacy-policy.html
Encryption: only standard OS networking is used; no custom encryption (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO).
